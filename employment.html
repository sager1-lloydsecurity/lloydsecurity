Technician

Help bring dependable security systems to life for the homes and businesses we serve.

As a Technician, you’ll work with the Lloyd team to install, service, and support security systems in the field. You’ll solve practical problems, learn evolving technology, and help customers feel confident in the systems protecting what matters to them.

This is a hands-on opportunity for someone who takes pride in careful work, clear communication, and leaving every site better than they found it.

Apply for this position ↗

Security Technician

Lloyd Security is a local, fast-paced security innovator serving the Twin Cities area. We design, install, service, and monitor residential and commercial systems for those seeking security solutions. We pride ourselves on going beyond expectations for our customers while delivering a great culture and opportunity for our team.

Technician job overview

We are looking for an eager, friendly, and efficient installation technician to join our growing team. The Security Installation Technician is responsible for tasks associated with the design, installation, upgrade, and repair service of home and small to mid-sized commercial security systems. Our Technicians use communication, technical knowledge, problem-solving, and sales skills to deliver the highest level of customer service. The right candidate will receive hands-on training from our skilled technicians.

Essential job functions

  • Perform system installation, upgrades, takeovers, and wiring for cameras, access, intercom, and intrusion systems.
  • Perform system replacements, repairs, and disconnects.
  • Perform signal verification, system testing, and troubleshooting.
  • Assist customers with downloads and device configurations as needed.
  • Educate and train customers on system operation and functionality.
  • Complete installation checklists and capture customer signatures as required.
  • Manage individual truck stock inventory, materials, tools, and company equipment.
  • Return defective and excess equipment to warehouse inventory.
  • Self-monitor performance through scorecard reports.
  • Acknowledge, implement, and enforce company policies and procedures.
  • Positively represent Lloyd Security, Inc. to customers with consistent professionalism and adherence to dress code.
  • Submit accurate and timely documentation to your immediate supervisor.
  • Work required on-call duty to handle after-hours service issues.

Qualifications / knowledge, skills & abilities

  • Basic reading, writing, and arithmetic skills required.
  • Ability to be physically fit, climb and stoop, and lift up to 40 lbs.
  • High school diploma or equivalent.
  • Valid state driver’s license with a clean driving record.
  • Ability to work independently and remain focused in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Ability to troubleshoot and conduct diagnostics to successfully problem-solve under pressure.
  • Motivated team player with a positive attitude and desire to learn and deliver high-quality work.
  • Strong customer service skills, genuine desire to interact with customers, and professional appearance.
  • PLT license and/or technical school training preferred.
  • Must pass a background check and drug test.

Compensation: Competitive hourly wage (BOE) plus upsell commission opportunities.

Benefits: Health, dental, group life, and retirement plan offered.

Please email a cover letter, resume, and three references to opportunities@lloydsecurity.com.

Accounting Associate

Keep the details moving so the rest of the team can do their best work.

As an Accounting Associate, you’ll support the day-to-day financial work that keeps Lloyd Security organized and growing. You’ll help maintain accurate records, support invoices and reconciliations, and work with teammates across the company.

This role is a strong fit for someone who is dependable, detail-oriented, and energized by making complex work feel clear and manageable.

Apply for this position ↗

Accounting Associate Overview

The Finance Manager reports directly to the General Manager and communicates and collaborates with the President, Sales Manager, and Operations Manager as needed. This position is responsible for day-to-day financial needs, including Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, customer billing inquiries, collections, job and service billing, audits, taxes, and financial reporting. This position requires specific metrics to be met and presented to the Leadership team during weekly and quarterly meetings.

Essential job functions

  • Enter and pay invoices through Accounts Payable.
  • Process customer payments through Accounts Receivable.
  • Act as the point of communication for customer billing questions.
  • Assist with answering inbound phone calls.
  • Manage collections.
  • Complete monthly reconciliation of vendor accounts.
  • Perform financial audits.
  • Pay sales and use tax.
  • Complete job and service billing.
  • Support month-end and year-end accounting.
  • Prepare financial reporting.
  • Complete additional job duties as required.

Qualifications / knowledge, skills & abilities

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance or Accounting, or equivalent experience.
  • Ability to work independently.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Ability to remain focused and effectively multitask within a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Motivated team player with a positive attitude and desire to learn and deliver high-quality work.
  • Strong customer service skills, genuine desire to interact with customers, and professional appearance.
  • Highly organized with strong attention to detail and follow-through.
  • Experience in the security industry preferred, but not required.
  • Must pass a background, drug, and reference check.

Please email a cover letter, resume, and three references to opportunities@lloydsecurity.com.
