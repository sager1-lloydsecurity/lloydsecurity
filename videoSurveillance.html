What’s Included in Your Commercial Video Surveillance System

A generic, self-installed camera system has limitations, such as blind spots, poor image quality, insecure data storage and a lack of integration options, leaving your business vulnerable. Lloyd Security offers comprehensive surveillance system installations and a suite of premium services we can integrate, which include:

01

High-Definition Commercial Video Surveillance Cameras

A challenge with many cameras is their low-quality picture and direct connection to a recorder, which can be sabotaged. We offer commercial-grade high-definition Internet Protocol (IP) cameras that deliver a clear picture and are network-connected, accessible, manageable and viewable from a connected device with the right credentials.

02

Centralized Monitoring and Cloud-Based Storage

Your recordings will be securely stored off-site using our cloud-based storage. Our systems also feature remote access and event verification, allowing our operators to view your facility if an alarm is triggered. You’ll know when something is happening and have footage as evidence to hand to the police if necessary.

You’ll receive regular updates on the condition of your cameras as well, and we’ll provide ongoing preventive maintenance to ensure the cameras are in top condition.

03

Integration with Access Control and Alarm Systems

We can integrate your new surveillance system with other security measures, such as intrusion-detection and fire alarm systems, as well as access control. These cameras become the heart of a unified security ecosystem, giving you peace of mind that your facility is safe.
