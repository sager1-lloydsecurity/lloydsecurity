Support / batteries

Keep the signal
strong.

A beeping panel often needs attention, and a low-battery alert is one of the most common reasons. Start with the guide for your panel above, then follow the steps for your specific 2 Gig system.

01

Identify the device

Confirm whether the alert is coming from the main panel, a keypad, or a wireless sensor before changing anything.

02

Use the right guide

GC3 and GC2 battery procedures are different. Use the matching panel reference above and keep the system’s instructions close by.

03

Still hearing a beep?

If the alert continues after following the guide, contact Lloyd Security so we can help diagnose the issue and protect your system’s monitoring.
