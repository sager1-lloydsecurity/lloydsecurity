How it works

What Is a Commercial Access Control System?

A commercial access control system helps businesses manage who enters and exits their building through cards, keypads or other authentication data. With a commercial access control system, you can easily log suspicious activity or integrate your system with other solutions, like an intrusion alarm, for greater protection.

What Makes up Security System Management Services for Businesses?

Commercial security management systems use door access control systems that involve software and hardware components that work together so employees or customers can enter and exit your building. Every commercial access control system consists of the following elements:

  • Control panel: The control panel is the heart of any access system, storing all the data it needs to function. Control panels also contain software that processes the input for door readers and credentials to send the command to unlock or lock the door.
  • Door readers: Door readers are at entry points and scan the card or other credentials presented before sending this information to the control panel.
  • Credentials: Credentials are the elements that grant people access to your building. A credential can be a key fob, mobile app or PIN code. Some companies also add an extra layer of security by requiring two-factor identification.
  • Lock hardware: The lock hardware works with the control panel and door reader to let people in and out of the building. Once the control panel receives the correct authorization, the lock hardware will disengage, and the door will open.
  • Management software: Management software allows businesses to have greater control over user access, activity and the types of alerts they receive so they can secure their building more effectively.
