Initial and ongoing process

Security is
an ongoing process.

We consider your safety an ongoing process that consists of four steps.

01

Assessment

Our commercial security solutions begin with a conversation about your needs. During your first contact with us, we’ll provide a quote and evaluate your premises. Our security experts will review your site and buildings and make commercial security system recommendations based on our findings.

02

Installation

After you choose the right commercial security solution for your business, our technicians will come to your site to install it. Our security experts will handle every aspect of installation so you can rest assured that your system will work as intended.

03

Monitoring

Once you have your security system on-premises, we can also oversee your security monitoring. We offer around-the-clock protection from our central monitoring station, where agents will notify the authorities immediately if they notice suspicious activity.

04

Service and maintenance

If you need security system troubleshooting or maintenance, we provide ongoing solutions to keep it at top performance. We also perform seamless transfers for customers who need to expand or move their security.
